# Logo Color Palette

## Primary Colors

| Color | HEX | RGB | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep Green | `#0A382E` | `10, 56, 46` | Main background |
| Mint Green | `#90CCA8` | `144, 204, 168` | Main logo/icon |
| Off White | `#F6FBFA` | `246, 251, 250` | White details |
| Medium Green | `#6FA58C` | `111, 165, 140` | Secondary elements |
| Dark Teal | `#2E5B48` | `46, 91, 72` | Shadows / accents |
| Muted Green | `#537E6B` | `83, 126, 107` | Supporting accent |

## Recommended Brand Palette

- **Primary:** `#0A382E`
- **Secondary:** `#90CCA8`
- **Light:** `#F6FBFA`
- **Accent:** `#6FA58C`

## Brand Direction

The strongest combination is **Deep Green + Mint Green + Off White**.  
This palette creates a natural, fresh, eco-friendly, clean, and premium visual identity.
